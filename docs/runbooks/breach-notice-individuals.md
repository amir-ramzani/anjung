# Notice to affected individuals (template, English and Malay)

> Counsel must review before first use. Send through the landlord's organisation where Anjung is the processor (decision D1). Keep it short and plain: what happened, what data, what they can do, who to contact. Do not speculate, do not blame, do not include more personal data than needed (no identity numbers, not even masked, in the message body).

Send by email to the address on file, and add an in-app notice. Record who was sent which version and when.

---

## English

**Subject:** Important: a security incident that affected some of your information

Dear [first name],

We are writing to tell you about a security incident involving personal information held for your tenancy at [property].

**What happened.** On [date] we found that [one or two plain sentences]. We [stopped it / fixed the cause] on [date].

**What information was involved.** [e.g. your name, email address and phone number.] [Your identity document number / payment card details were / were not involved.]

**What this could mean for you.** [e.g. you may receive scam messages that appear to come from your landlord or from us.]

**What we have done.** [Contained the issue, secured the accounts, told the Personal Data Protection Commissioner / told your landlord.]

**What you can do.**
- Be careful with emails, calls or messages asking for money or personal details, even if they seem to come from us or your landlord. We will never ask for your password or a payment outside the payment page you use for your rent.
- [If relevant: change your password; we have signed you out everywhere.]
- [If identity details were involved: watch for unusual activity and tell us if anything looks wrong.]

**Who to contact.** [Name or team], [email], [phone], [hours]. You can also contact the Personal Data Protection Commissioner.

We are sorry that this happened. We will tell you if we learn anything that changes the above.

[Sender name, role]
[Organisation]

## Bahasa Malaysia

**Subjek:** Penting: insiden keselamatan yang menjejaskan sebahagian maklumat anda

Kepada [nama pertama],

Kami menulis untuk memaklumkan anda tentang insiden keselamatan yang melibatkan maklumat peribadi yang disimpan berkaitan penyewaan anda di [hartanah].

**Apa yang berlaku.** Pada [tarikh] kami mendapati bahawa [satu atau dua ayat ringkas]. Kami telah [menghentikannya / membetulkan puncanya] pada [tarikh].

**Maklumat yang terlibat.** [cth. nama, alamat e-mel dan nombor telefon anda.] [Nombor dokumen pengenalan / butiran kad pembayaran anda [tidak] terlibat.]

**Apa kesannya kepada anda.** [cth. anda mungkin menerima mesej penipuan yang kelihatan datang daripada tuan rumah anda atau daripada kami.]

**Apa yang telah kami lakukan.** [Mengawal isu tersebut, mengamankan akaun, memaklumkan Pesuruhjaya Perlindungan Data Peribadi / memaklumkan tuan rumah anda.]

**Apa yang boleh anda lakukan.**
- Berhati-hati dengan e-mel, panggilan atau mesej yang meminta wang atau maklumat peribadi, walaupun nampak seperti datang daripada kami atau tuan rumah anda. Kami tidak akan meminta kata laluan anda atau pembayaran di luar halaman pembayaran yang anda gunakan untuk sewa.
- [Jika berkaitan: tukar kata laluan anda; kami telah mengeluarkan anda daripada semua peranti.]
- [Jika butiran pengenalan terlibat: peka terhadap aktiviti luar biasa dan beritahu kami jika ada yang mencurigakan.]

**Hubungi siapa.** [Nama atau pasukan], [e-mel], [telefon], [waktu]. Anda juga boleh menghubungi Pesuruhjaya Perlindungan Data Peribadi.

Kami memohon maaf atas perkara ini. Kami akan memaklumkan anda jika terdapat perkembangan yang mengubah maklumat di atas.

[Nama penghantar, jawatan]
[Organisasi]
